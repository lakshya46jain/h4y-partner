# H4Y Partner

This mobile app is designed and developed using Flutter to provide a seamless and user-friendly experience for professionals across various devices. The app serves as the professional’s interface to manage bookings, interact with customers, and manage their services, all in one place. With an intuitive design, the app helps professionals streamline their workflow and enhance their client interactions.

## Key Features

- **Booking Management**: Professionals can view a list of incoming bookings, with the option to accept or reject them based on availability and preferences.

- **Customer Communication**: A built-in chat feature enables direct communication between professionals and customers, making it easier to address queries, discuss requirements, and finalize details.

- **Service Management**: Professionals can create and manage the services they offer, customize their service listings, and set their own pricing, ensuring flexibility and control over their offerings.

This app empowers professionals to stay organized, efficiently manage bookings, and maintain clear communication with their customers, all while having full control over their service listings.
